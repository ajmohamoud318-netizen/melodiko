# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pre-schoolers aged 3–6, playing independently on a phone or tablet (primarily iOS). The child is the primary user — not the parent. Interactions must work with chubby fingers, short attention spans, and zero reading ability. Parents may hand the device to the child; the app must be self-explanatory from first glance.

## Product Purpose

Piano Fun! is an interactive kids' piano app. Children pick a song from the song picker, then play the melody on a colour-coded piano keyboard. The app guides them through nursery rhymes (Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Lamb, Happy Birthday, Hot Cross Buns) using highlighted keys. Success is feeling music come alive under their fingers.

## Brand Personality

Adventurous, magical, warm. Each song is a tiny world with its own character. The fish swims away, the keys light up, sounds reward every tap. The app should feel like a storybook that plays music — not a learning drill.

## Anti-references

- Generic edu-app aesthetic: primary-color grids, Comic Sans, clip-art icons, flat rainbow palettes.
- Loud and overstimulating: multiple blinking UI elements competing at once; notification-style reward spam.
- Corporate or sterile: cold minimalism, adult-app whitespace, muted neutrals, "clean" in a way that reads as empty to a child.

## Design Principles

1. **Every tap is an event** — interactions reward the child with motion, sound, or colour, not just state change.
2. **Characters over chrome** — the fish, the sheep, the cake are the UI. Lean into illustration; reduce generic widget use.
3. **One thing at a time** — the screen has one clear action available. Never compete for attention.
4. **Delight before legibility** — a 4-year-old can't read "Twinkle Twinkle" but they recognise a fish. Lead with imagery.
5. **Generous targets** — tap areas must be large enough for small hands with low motor precision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Reduced-motion: honour `prefers-reduced-motion` — skip swim/wink animations, use instant transitions.
- Touch targets: minimum 44×44px interactive areas everywhere.
- Colour: do not rely on colour alone to communicate state (key highlights should also have shape/size change).
- Audio: the app is inherently audio-first; no silent-mode dependency.
